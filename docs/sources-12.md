# Level 12 · Lights Out — sources for "What actually happened…"

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| 23 December 2015, from about 15:35 local time; three oblenergos; about 225,000 customers; spear phishing with Office documents carrying BlackEnergy 3 (spring 2015); credentials harvested; VPN and existing remote administration tools; operators locked out; breakers opened with the SCADA HMIs, at least 27 substations; malicious firmware on serial-to-Ethernet devices; modified KillDisk; a UPS reconfigured; thousands of calls to call centres; manual operation; constrained mode afterwards | E-ISAC and SANS ICS, "Analysis of the Cyber Attack on the Ukrainian Power Grid", Defense Use Case, 18 March 2016 |
| Company names (Prykarpattyaoblenergo, Kyivoblenergo, Chernivtsioblenergo); outages of 1 to 6 hours; attribution to Sandworm by researchers | Wikipedia (citing Wired / Kim Zetter 2016, Reuters 2016, Ukrainian Ministry of Energy 2016) |
| 19 October 2020: DOJ charges six GRU Unit 74455 officers, including for "December 2015 through December 2016 destructive malware attacks against Ukraine's electric power grid" with BlackEnergy, Industroyer and KillDisk | US Department of Justice press release |
