Butterfly effects (attacker minutes count while the registers are not contained or the vendor account is open; the jump to December 12 counts too):
- 40 attacker minutes without opening alert #10 → the security vendor escalates it and nobody answers (the level's title).
- 90 minutes, registers cleaned but the vendor account still open → the registers are re-infected through it.
- 120 minutes with live or re-infected registers → card data is collected and leaves the network; on December 12 the bank reports cards for sale in bulk (−10 points, so mass card theft never passes). If alert #13 was opened in triage, the outbound route is blocked and only a small batch leaves.
