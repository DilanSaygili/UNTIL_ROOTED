# Level 5 · The Alarm Nobody Answered — sources for "What actually happened…"

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Credentials stolen from Fazio Mechanical Services (HVAC/refrigeration contractor, Pennsylvania), reportedly via malware-infected email, at least two months before the breach | US Senate Commerce Committee staff report, "A 'Kill Chain' Analysis of the 2013 Target Data Breach" (March 26, 2014) |
| First access to Target's network around November 12, 2013 (the report cites the New York Times) | Senate staff report |
| POS malware on a few registers November 15–28; most registers infected by November 30 | Senate staff report |
| FireEye alerts November 30, December 2 and 3; Symantec flagged activity around November 28; automatic deletion not enabled; no response | Senate staff report |
| Exfiltration from December 2, about 11 GB over two weeks | Senate staff report |
| DOJ contacted Target December 12; malware mostly removed December 15 | Senate staff report |
| Krebs reported December 18; Target confirmed December 19 (~40 million card accounts); January 10, 2014: up to 70 million customers' personal data | Senate staff report |
| PCI DSS certification in September 2013 | Senate staff report (Target CFO testimony) |
| $18.5 million settlement with 47 states and the District of Columbia (May 2017) | BankInfoSecurity, May 24, 2017 |
